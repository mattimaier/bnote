# User Guide Ownership

## Page Ownership Model

- **Product owner**: validates value proposition, migration messaging, and release accuracy.
- **Engineering owner**: validates behavior, feature limits, and known caveats.
- **Language reviewers**:
  - DE lead reviewer
  - EN lead reviewer
  - ES reviewer
  - FR reviewer

## Ownership by Section

- **Getting Started**: Product + DE/EN reviewers
- **Features**: Product + Engineering
- **Admin**: Engineering + Product
- **What's New**: Product first draft, Engineering validation
- **Migration**: Product + Engineering joint approval
- **Troubleshooting**: Engineering owner
- **Why Next Gen**: Product owner

## Definition of Done

Each page update is complete only when:

1. User language is clear for non-technical readers
2. DE and EN are reviewed
3. ES and FR parity exists for critical pages
4. Links are valid
5. Relevant "What's New" and migration links are cross-referenced
