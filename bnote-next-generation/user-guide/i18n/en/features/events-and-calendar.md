# Events and calendar: planning without chaos

Events and calendar help your band stay aligned on time, place, and participation.

## What belongs here

- rehearsals
- concerts
- related schedule details

## Practical planning flow

1. Create or open the event
2. Confirm date, time, and location
3. Confirm participant list
4. Trigger participation responses
5. Share one clear update in comments

## Why this is better

Instead of mixing planning across multiple chat threads, you keep event context in one place.

## Quick quality check before each rehearsal

- Is the location clear?
- Is the participant list complete?
- Are responses mostly complete?
- Is there one final update message?
