# Participation: who can attend and who cannot

Participation is one of the most important workflows in BNote Next Generation.

## What this feature does

For each event, members can respond:

- Yes
- Maybe
- No

You can then see who is confirmed, uncertain, or still pending.

## Why it matters

Most rehearsal stress comes from unclear attendance.
This feature gives one clear answer list for planning.

## How to use it (simple)

1. Open the event
2. Request responses from participants
3. Watch pending responses
4. Follow up only with people who have not answered
5. Finalize planning based on confirmed participants

## Visual clues that help

- Colors show response status quickly
- Overview bars show totals by status
- Participant grouping helps section-level planning

## If you used old BNote

The core idea is the same, but the response status is easier to read and act on quickly.
